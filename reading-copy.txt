PHOTOGRAPH 07 — IMG_0910.HEIC
Arrival at Suez and passage to Kantara
=================================================================

Left page

May 3rd, Saturday, 4 pm: arrived at Port Tewfik. We pass desolated shores up the coast on the opposite Arabian side of the Red Sea. We are now [illegible] or 3rd, owing to the time difference. So on Saturday, [inserted: 4th] Sunday, we are anchored in Suez. The Mary is with us. She is as [still?] packed with troops. A destroyer is parked between us. A whole nest of shipping is at port, anchored apart.

We are told we are going to Palestine. I can see the Suez Canal entrance; we may go down it some distance yet. [Very?] hot in the Q E. Suez is a burnt-up looking port. Opposite us here, there is a big sandstone bare mound of rock [rears?] above us. [illegible: approximately 4 words] off this morning for different ports; it will be [illegible] day.

The boys start disembarking about 1:30 pm, down the gangway into three large punts. Some pelting back hard-boiled eggs that had been included in their ration box.

5/5/41, Monday. [About 500?] are to disembark this morning. 2/9th have already pulled off [illegible] large barge [illegible] this morning.

Right page

Many more men are lined up on deck, awaiting to go down the gangway. I am troop orderly sergeant. Breakfast time for those not getting off has been advanced slightly. Remaining will not come until 9 am. I understand we are going off tomorrow. Not eager to leave the good ship. The voyage of cabin comfort and messing excellence will live long in my memory.

The lads are being unloaded into punts, then transferred to smaller ships which I understand are to proceed down the Suez Canal some few miles, where the lads will be entrained at Kantara East.

Tuesday, 6/5/41: we disembark from Q E at 6 am and re-embark on small ship Ethiopia for dis[illegible] along the canal. Arrived at El Kantara about 4 pm, disembarked, and had a feed of sausages, spuds and peas. Entrained at 5:30 am on 7/5/41, Wednesday. Passed through Romani, El Arish, etc. Many old wire entanglements still remained.

Right page—journey record

Embarked Sydney, 9/4/41. Left Sydney on Queen Elizabeth, 11/4/41. Accompanied Australia, Mauretania, Nieuw Amsterdam, Ile de France, Queen Mary. Arrived Fremantle, 16/4/41; left Fremantle, 19/4/41.

Anzac Day: 10-minute silence. Burial at sea, member of crew. Arrived Trincomalee, Ceylon, Saturday, 26/4/41, at 6:30 am. Very pretty port, surrounded by green foliage, hills, palm avenues, etc. Not allowed on shore. Remained at this place all day Sunday. Very hot [oppressive?]. [Natives?] [illegible] mast [fittings?] for dive bombers.

HMAS Canberra escorts Q E and Q M out of port for sea at 4 pm, 27/4/41. 1st May: sighted land on starboard side, a strange ship. 2/5/41: passed Aden at about 9 pm (local time).

May 3rd, Saturday: arrived Port Tewfik. (Saturday night till Monday) at anchor in Suez. On 6/5/41 we disembarked from Q.E. to small ship Ethiopia. We came down Suez Canal to Kantara, we disembark and are taken to receiving depot that night. Early on 7/5/41 we are entrained for Gaza, arriving about 3 pm. Passed through Romani and El Arish.
